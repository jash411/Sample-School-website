Our Academic Programs

Grade 9

Our Grade 9 program focuses on foundational knowledge and skills development, preparing students for higher secondary education with a strong emphasis on core subjects and character building.

  • Core subjects: Math, English, Science
  • Introduction to high school life
  • Spiritual development programs

Grade 10

Grade 10 students begin to specialize while maintaining a broad curriculum. We emphasize critical thinking and prepare students for national examinations.

  • Subject specialization begins
  • Career guidance programs
  • Community service initiatives

Grade 11

Our Grade 11 program offers advanced coursework and preparation for university entrance, with increased focus on research and independent study skills.

  • Advanced subject options
  • Research projects
  • Leadership development

Grade 12

The final year focuses on university preparation, national examinations, and transitioning to adulthood with our comprehensive senior program.

  • University preparation
  • Exam-focused curriculum
  • Life skills training
